How it works

01

Point & Detect

Point your camera at the grass, rough, or other search area. ForeAI automatically highlights detected golf balls.

02

Lock the Marker

Once a ball is detected, tap lock to keep the marker anchored on the ball while you retrieve it.

03

Take Photos

Take a photo to analyze it with ForeAI's highest-accuracy model and inspect the results in detail.

04

Get the Best Results

Search in good lighting where the ball is visible when possible. Tall grass, shadows, and long distances may reduce detection accuracy.
